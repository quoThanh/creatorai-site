Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

1. Whose data

CreatorAI is used only by its operator with his own TikTok account. It does not collect data about other TikTok users.

2. What data the app accesses

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar of the operator's account.
  • Account stats (user.info.stats): follower, following, like and video counts of the operator's account.
  • Video list (video.list): the operator's own public videos and their view, like, comment and share counts.
  • Posting (video.upload, video.publish): used only to upload videos the operator has approved.

3. How data is stored

Access tokens are encrypted and stored only on the operator's own computer. Video statistics are stored in a local database on the same computer. Nothing is stored on this website; this site only hosts information pages and a sign-in return page that runs entirely in the browser.

4. Sharing

Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

5. Deleting data and revoking access

The operator can revoke the app's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services, and can delete the local database and token file from his computer.
