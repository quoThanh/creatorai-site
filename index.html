CreatorAI

CreatorAI is a personal, non-commercial tool built by one developer for his own TikTok account (@nhatkycoder53, "Nhật Ký Coder 53"). It is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) so the owner can authorize access to his own account.
  • Uploads videos the owner has reviewed and approved to his own account (Content Posting API). Every post is confirmed by the owner before it is sent.
  • Reads basic profile information and the view, like, comment and share counts of the owner's own videos, so he can learn which content is useful to viewers.

What it does not do

  • It does not access other people's accounts, send messages, follow, like or comment automatically.
  • It does not sell or share any data.
